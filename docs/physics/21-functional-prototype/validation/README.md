## 3. Derivations (generator)

- **Slider-crank** (VC-10…12): r = 3.875 in / 2 = 49.2125 mm (the O-320 stroke [C-R20], used only as a realistic
  size). l = 150 mm is **stated test geometry**, since rod lengths are not public (research note C §3).
  x = r(1 − cos θ) + l(1 − √(1 − λ² sin² θ)), T = F·dx/dθ, F_rod = F / cos β, F_side = F tan β, sin β = λ sin θ
  [C-R12]. The generator evaluates both torque forms (F·dx/dθ and F r sin(θ+β)/cos β) and they agree to 1e-12.
- **Mean torque** (VC-15): BMEP = P·2·60 / (V_d·rpm) [C-R18] = 73.5 kW·120 / (1.352e-3 m³·5800) = 11.2477 bar,
  T_brake = BMEP·V_d / (4π) = 121.013 N·m. With losses modelled (FR-FPR-71), the heat release is calibrated to this
  **brake** value, so IMEP = BMEP + FMEP. The match with the datasheet's 121 N·m is by construction (`calibrated`),
  not validation evidence.
- **Gear** (VC-16, 27): 5800 · 21/51 (teeth [E-R4]; the datasheet's 2.43 is the rounded ratio [C-R23]). Torque
  121 · 51/21 · 0.99 [E-R21].
- **Inertia torque** (VC-28): at constant ω the governor supplies T = m·ẍ·x′ = m·ω²·x″(θ)·x′(θ), with x′ exact and
  x″ by central differences on the exact x′ (h = 1e-5 rad, truncation ~1e-10). Sign: positive = the governor
  drives. Cross-check: the series form in note E §3.1 gives −79.5 N·m at 30° (torque *on* the crank, opposite
  sign), within its truncation error of 2.6 %. The rod mass is set to 1 g with negligible inertia, so the case
  isolates the piston.
- **Friction terms** (VC-29): note E §1.3, reconstructed PNH forms, with S̄_p = 2·S·n/60 = 11.79 m/s, p_i/p_a = 1 and
  r_c = 10.8 [E-R2]. Grade C until the forms are checked against the SAE originals (TECHNICAL_SPEC §11 Q8).
- **Reference BMEP** (VC-31): BMEP = 4π·T/V_d with the torque digitised from OM-912 Fig. 4 [E-R2] (±1 N·m reading
  ≈ ±0.09 bar). The peak of 128 N·m at 5100 rpm agrees with the datasheet [E-R1].
- **Clearance** (VC-18/19): the bar's farthest-x points are the corners (100, ±5). At rotation φ, max x =
  100 cos φ + 5|sin φ|. Its maximum is √(100² + 5²) = 100.12492 mm at φ = ±atan(5/100) = ±2.8624°, so the
  clearance is 105 − 100.12492 = 4.87508 mm. Contact with the x = 95 face holds while 100 cos φ + 5|sin φ| ≥ 95,
  i.e. |φ| ≤ 21.2735° (bisection).
- **Mobility** (VC-20): Gruebler = 6·3 − Σ constraints [A-R59]. Rank: 3 tree coordinates. The planar loop's
  out-of-plane position row and both axis rows have zero derivative, so the loop rank is 2 and DOF = 3 − 2 = 1.
  Gruebler alone cannot prove redundancy [A-R60].

## 4. Reference engine (VC-24, production-gate scenario)

**MVP: single-cylinder demonstrator.** One cylinder of the Rotax 912 (338 cm³):

- bore 84 mm and stroke 61 mm [C-R23];
- compression ratio 10.8 [E-R2];
- rod 130 mm, from one low-quality paper [E-R8], so `unverified`;
- 51/21 reduction [E-R4] at η 0.99 [E-R21];
- flywheel;
- two-blade propeller with the shipped C_T(J), C_P(J) table and an inertia inside 0.15–0.60 kg·m² [E-R4];
- materials: `al_4032` piston [E-R32], `steel_4340` crank, rod and pin [E-R31];
- losses: `fmep_table` or `chen_flynn` with a stated source until §11 Q8 is resolved;
- combustion: `single_zone` calibrated to the per-cylinder brake share of the rated point (BMEP 11.2477 bar at
  5800 rpm).

Validation in MVP is per BMEP (displacement-normalised), against VC-31 at the hold-out rpm. Away from the calibration
point a miss is expected without a breathing model (FUNCTIONAL_SPEC FR-FPR-78); the result records it as information,
not as a model defect.
Parts:

- crankcase with a cylinder bore and main bearing bores (fixed body);
- crankshaft with journals, web and crank pin;
- connecting rod with big-end and small-end bores;
- piston with a wrist-pin boss;
- wrist pin;
- gear pair on parallel shafts;
- propeller shaft with hub and blades of real thickness.

Every joint is realised by a modelled pin and bore with a 0.02 mm diametral clearance. The plan lives in
`plans/reference_single_cylinder.plan.json` (written at M4, when the commands exist). Expected:

- `check_assembly`: no fail, all joints realised;
- `inspect_dynamics`: DOF 1;
- the run settles to a governed or loaded speed;
- loop residual ≤ 1e-6 m and energy closure ≤ 0.5 %;
- `check_motion_clearance`: no collision over one cycle;
- every output has an accuracy statement (VC-34); timestep halving ≤ 1 % (VC-33);
- verdict `ready_for_review`.

**Seeded-defect corpus** (KPI): copies of the reference plan with one defect each:

- the rod overlaps the crank web;
- a blade has zero thickness;
- the spinner floats;
- the propeller hub is made of two lumps;
- a 0.3 mm wall in the crankcase;
- the crank pin has no bore on the rod;
- the wrist pin is moved 1 mm.

Each must produce exactly its code.

**V1:** a four-cylinder horizontally opposed Rotax-912-class engine, firing order 1-4-2-3 [E-R2]. It is compared
against the whole full-throttle curve (VC-31) and, with a 1.70–1.80 m fixed-pitch propeller, against the static rpm
window (VC-32).

## 5. What is not validated

- The shape of the torque curve away from the calibration points: there is no breathing model (V2).
- Friction components separately: no FMEP measurement exists for these engines [E §5.4]. Only the total is
  constrained, through the brake calibration.
- Torsional vibration, dog-hub impacts and resonances: rigid bodies cannot represent them [E §3.2].

- Real cylinder-pressure traces of a named aviation engine: none was found (research note C §4).
- Measured bearing loads: none found. The reactions are proven only against the analytic slider-crank (VC-11/12).
- Strength of the parts: out of scope (05 FEA, PHY-05/07).

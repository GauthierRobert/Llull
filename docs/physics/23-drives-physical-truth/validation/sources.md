## Verified derivations (grade A, `generate_drv_reference.py`)

- DC motor: exact 2×2 linear solution (eigenvalues −253.666, −3170.58 s⁻¹ for R1 data). An energy identity is checked inside the
  generator: input − copper − kinetic − magnetic = −4.7e-13 J over 30 ms.
- Thermal: 2-node network solved through its eigen decomposition, plus the closed-form fixed point with copper α.
- Lead screw, gear spin-up, inertia-matching ratio: power-balance derivations in the docstrings.
- Stick-slip limit cycle: piecewise closed form (docstring). Self-check: slip ends at x = 2x_e − x_s (−1.7e-18 m vs 0).
- Cam-law coefficients: cycloidal, 3-4-5 and harmonic in closed form. Modified trapezoid and modified sine come from the stated
  acceleration shapes integrated on 200 000 steps, which gives 4.88812 / 5.52796 (C_a) and 2.0 / 1.75960 (C_v). These match the
  commonly tabulated 4.888 / 5.528 / 1.76.
- PMSM envelope (R = 0), induction equivalent circuit, stepper small-angle frequency, hydraulic orifice, spring-damper stop restitution.

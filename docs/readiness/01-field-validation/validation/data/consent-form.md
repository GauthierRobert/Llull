## Why

To find out whether engineers can do their daily work in llull, and to fix what stops them. Your answers and timings are
used for this purpose only (Art. 5(1)(b)) and are reported only in aggregate, without your name.

## What we collect, and what we never collect

| Collected | Never collected |
| --------- | --------------- |
| your answers to questionnaires (SUS, task difficulty, workload) and the interview notes | your drawings, models, survey files, client names, coordinates, file names |
| task times and outcomes noted by the moderator | screen recordings (unless you tick the separate box below) |
| only if you turn it on and choose to send the file: the local usage log (names of the commands used, durations, error codes, undo counts) | anything sent automatically by the software: the software sends nothing |

You are identified by a code such as `P-CIV-03`. The key linking the code to your name is kept by the pilot lead,
separately, and is deleted at the end date. Coded data are still personal data (Recital 26 [R10]).

## Your choices

- Taking part is voluntary. Your relationship with us, including any licence, does not depend on it (Art. 7(4)).
- You can withdraw at any time, as easily as you agreed: tell the moderator, or e-mail [address]. Turning the usage log off
  in Help › Pilot diagnostics stops it immediately, and *Delete* removes it from your machine (Art. 7(3)).
- Withdrawal does not affect data already used in aggregate before it (Art. 7(3)). On request we delete your coded
  records that are not yet aggregated.
- You can ask for access, correction or erasure of your data at [address], and you can complain to the authority above.

## Storage and end date

Data are stored on [location: the pilot lead's encrypted drive; no cloud service]. All raw data and the code key are
deleted on [end date], which is also the end date entered in the usage-log consent (Art. 5(1)(e)).

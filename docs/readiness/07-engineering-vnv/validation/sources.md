Reading for the spec (not legal advice): Belgian law places professional liability for intellectual services on the
architect / engineer / other service provider who performs them, and obliges them to be insured. A software tool is not
a party in that scheme; the signing professional is. llull's report therefore supports a signature, it never replaces one
(FR-VNV-110, FR-VNV-115).
